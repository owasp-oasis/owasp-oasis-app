ResponseBadgeCard — preview-branch recognition card (Badges tab). Shows meaning, earned state, and progress on EVERY criterion separately.
```jsx
<ResponseBadgeCard name="Fast Responder" kind="Activity badge" earned={false} description="…" criteria={[{label:'Recognized responses (90d)',value:7,target:10},{label:'Within 24h',value:86,target:80,unit:'%'}]}/>
```
Badges never imply expertise and never affect reputation or vote weight.
