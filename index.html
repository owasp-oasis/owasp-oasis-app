The mechanism

AI brings speed. You bring expertise.

OASIS is the community validation layer between fix automation and upstream maintainers. The work unit is small, focused, and useful: decide whether a candidate fix is real, credible, and worth advancing.

01

Generate candidate fixes

Fix automation tools produce candidate patches for known vulnerabilities in open source repositories.

02

Validate with humans

AppSec experts review the vulnerability and the proposed fix, separating credible signal from AI-generated noise.

03

Advance upstream

Maintainers receive vetted security signal while keeping full control over what fits and what merges.

Our ethos

Vendor-neutral, community-led.

OASIS belongs in the AppSec community. It treats fix automation as an enabler, not the authority. Credibility comes from transparent human validation and respect for open source maintainers.

Open culture

Security contribution should feel like a natural part of open source contribution, not an outside process imposed on maintainers.

Human judgment

Automation can move fast, but expert validators decide which fixes are real, credible, and useful.

Small actions

OASIS turns security expertise into focused validation work that can be done in minutes, without owning the whole remediation workflow.

Shared trust

Community standards make the process auditable and help maintainers receive signal instead of unvetted AI slop.
